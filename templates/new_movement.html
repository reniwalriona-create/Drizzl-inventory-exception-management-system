Log a stock movement

For anything with no paperwork: production runs, flea-market withdrawals, consignment transfers to a sales associate before any PO exists, or fixing a count. Movement types: production (new stock, no source location), opening balance (starting stock as of a given date -- use this instead of production so it isn't counted as newly manufactured), transfer (moves between locations, still Drizzl's), sale (leaves Drizzl's books), loss (damaged/expired/missing).
